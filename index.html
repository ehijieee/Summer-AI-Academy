Why it matters

The children who learn to build with AI early will have the advantage.

Safety & supervision

A safe, closed, supervised space.

Built into how the programme runs — not added as an afterthought.

  • Small, camera-on classes
  • No private one-to-one messaging between an adult and a child
  • Only enrolled students and the tutor in the workspace
  • Parents are told which platforms are used and are welcome to observe
  • A dedicated module on responsible, safe AI use for every age

A final showcase you can watch live.

On the last day, every team presents what they built — live and online, so you can join from work or home. You'll watch your child explain their project, demonstrate it, and answer questions. That moment is the real proof of what the programme delivered.
